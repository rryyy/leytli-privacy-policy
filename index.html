Leytli Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Leytli is a private place to write. Nobody reads what you write, including me, the developer. Your journal is kept on your phone, encrypted. There is no account, no server holding your notes, no analytics and no ads.

Leytli is made by John Ray Guerra in the Philippines. This policy covers the Leytli app on Android and iOS. It says what stays on your phone, the few things that leave it, and when.

What stays on your phone

  • Your notes, readings, kept sentences, places and photos are stored only on your phone, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. Its key is kept in your phone's secure keystore (the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain).
  • Readings (good, mixed or hard) are worked out on your phone by a language model that runs on the phone itself. What you write is never sent anywhere to be read.
  • Photos you take or pick are stripped of their location and other metadata, then stored encrypted on your phone. Picking a photo uses your phone's own photo picker, so Leytli sees only the photo you choose.
  • App lock uses your phone's own Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Leytli is told only whether you unlocked. It never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • Text you share into Leytli from another app, or write in a Quick note, stays on your phone like any other note.

What leaves your phone, and when

Only these things, and none of them carries what you wrote:

  • The reading model, downloaded once. The first time you save in Write, Leytli downloads its reading model (about 529 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, Hugging Face receives the request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Nothing you wrote is sent. You can remove the model at any time in Settings, under Remove downloaded model.
  • Place lookups you start yourself. When you tap Add a place, your phone's approximate position goes to Google (on Android) or Apple (on iOS) to find nearby place names. On iOS, a name you type to search for also goes to Apple. Leytli keeps only the name, town and province you choose, never coordinates, and never looks up a place unless you ask it to.
  • Backups you make. A backup is one file, encrypted with a passphrase only you know (AES-256-GCM), and saved wherever you choose. If you save it to a cloud drive, that service holds the encrypted file. Without your passphrase, nobody can open it, including me.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, a reminder about a sentence you kept shows that sentence. Until it fires, its text waits in your phone's notification system, on your phone only, and is left out of your phone's cloud backup. Leytli never schedules a reminder that quotes a note read as hard, and schedules none that quote you while App lock is on.

Your phone's own backup

If your phone's own backup is on, it may include Leytli's journal file, still encrypted. The key that opens it stays in this phone's secure keystore and is not copied, so that copy cannot be read on another phone. Photos and scheduled reminders are left out of it entirely. To move your journal to a new phone, use Leytli's own backup.

What I collect

Nothing. Leytli has no account and no server that receives your notes, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. I do not sell or share any data, because I hold none.

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics data, Google Play or the App Store may give me crash reports about the app. These come from the store, under its own privacy policy, and do not contain what you wrote.

Permissions

  • Location, approximate and only while you use the app: to list places near you when you tap Add a place.
  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for a day.
  • Notifications, and on Android alarms & reminders: to remind you at the times you set.
  • Face ID or fingerprint: only for App lock, if you turn it on.
  • Internet: only for the model download and the place lookups above.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your journal stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a single note, a photo or the reading model in the app, or everything at once with Reset everything in Settings. Uninstalling Leytli deletes the journal from the phone. Because I hold none of your data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete on your behalf. Under the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 those rights are yours, and you exercise them directly on your phone.

Children

Leytli is not made for children under 13.

Changes

If Leytli ever adds something that sends data off your phone, such as an optional sync, this policy will say so before that feature reaches you, and the date at the top will change.
